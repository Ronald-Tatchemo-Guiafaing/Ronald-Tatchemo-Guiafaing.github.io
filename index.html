About Me

I am an engineer drawn to medicine as a systems problem: noisy data, hard constraints, and decisions that must remain safe under real clinical pressure. My work sits where computer vision, machine learning, and embedded intelligence meet image-guided and surgical workflows, with the goal of tools that clinicians can trust.

I care about reproducible pipelines, constrained deployment (edge / TinyML), and data practices that respect privacy and integrity in care settings. Different training paths can meet on the same ground: improving how technology supports diagnosis, planning, and intervention.

Research direction

Dependable perception for continuum robots in interventional settings. The shape and the motion have to stay usable when a dedicated sensor, the endoscopic view, or the robot state is missing. I build the sensing and the software that recover that information for the clinician.

Focus

  • Surgical / medical AI and computer vision
  • Perception under clinical constraints
  • Biosignal modelling (e.g. sEMG) for surgical tools
  • Continuum-robot shape and motion when the endoscopic view or the robot state is missing
  • Deep learning on noisy time-series sensor streams
  • Passage classification under multipath, cross-reads and stationary tags
  • TinyML / FPGA for on-device intelligence
  • Trustworthy clinical data and hospital digitalization
