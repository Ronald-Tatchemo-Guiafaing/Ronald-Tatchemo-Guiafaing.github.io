About Me

I am an engineer drawn to medicine as a systems problem: noisy data, hard constraints, and decisions that must remain safe under real clinical pressure. My work sits where computer vision, machine learning, and embedded intelligence meet image-guided and surgical workflows, with the goal of tools that clinicians can trust.

I care about reproducible pipelines, constrained deployment (edge / TinyML), and data practices that respect privacy and integrity in care settings. Different training paths can meet on the same ground: improving how technology supports diagnosis, planning, and intervention.

Research direction

Dependable multimodal perception for surgical and interventional systems. Key gaps remain in intraoperative sensing and force awareness, especially when dedicated sensors, endoscopic vision, or robot-state signals are incomplete or unavailable, and when paired ground-truth data are scarce. I focus on reproducible software and modelling pipelines that combine computer vision, biosignals such as sEMG, and learning methods toward safer, more objective surgical assistance.
